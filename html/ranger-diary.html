My job

You might be wondering what I do here at Waxwing Basin National Park and if a career in forestry is for you!

In my job I wear a few different hats. I help plan and sometimes lead WBPA events, I monitor local wildlife populations and territory, and I help maintain the infrastructure including the visitors centre and those broadcasting stations you might see dotted around the forest.

What are those stations for? Well I’m not sure! That’s the job of some engineer or executive, I just check the power generators. But I bet their average work day isn’t as much fun as mine!

Nestled in the foothills of the Rocky Mountains, this humble national park doesn’t see the foot traffic of more popular locals like Banff and Lake Louise, but you might see that as a blessing once you’ve had a chance to experience our secluded little slice of paradise. Easy and intermediate walking trails interlace a dense and picturesque kingdom of Paper Birch and Douglas Firs which are home to the majestic Mule Deer and the eponymous Waxwing*. Call our main office line for details on renting the cozily rustic Waxwing Basin Lakeside Cabin** for a private event.

*Do not feed local wildlife.
**Homicide rumors are unsubstantiated and treated as urban legend by local authorities.
