Hampton's Haven

One month before my eighth birthday my family moved from England to Canada. They told me because Alberta is rat free I could not bring Hampton but he is my best friend so I lied and hid him inside a biscuit tin. They told me that rats are kept out by the Rat Petrol Man. I know what ‘petrol’ is it’s what daddy puts in our car to make it go.

I thought it would be okay to have Hampton with me in secret because he is just one rat and the prairie is very big. But one day I was playing outside because my mommy was telling me about how people are fixing the problems about the year 2000 insects and I got bored. When I was outside playing dolls the Petrol Man visited me. I knew it was him because there was petrol dripping all down him so much that I could not see his face. He said that he needed to take Hampton and I was scared because I wanted Hampton to be safe but he said Hampton could still be safe he just had to live together with the others.

So I took Hampton in his tin with me and followed the Petrol Man out into the field then down the stairs into a tunnel. It was dark but I wasn’t scared because I had Hampton to protect me. Then we finally got to a big cave and I met the Rat King. It was probably one million or more rats all tied together with their tails and I thought they were unhappy and felt scared because they looked like they were trying to get free but the Petrol Man said they are happy like this. He says it’s not true that Alberta has no rats it has only one rat because the Rat King just counts as one rat and it rules over all the rats in the world.

So I gave Hampton to him so that he could follow the law and the Petrol Man promised me he can join the Rat King and be happy like the others. I was sad because I thought I would never see Hampton again but the Petrol Man promised me that when I’m older he will come back and take me to see the Rat King again :)

This week we learned about ancient Egypt and I learned it isn’t prehistoric because they had their own language to rwrite stuff down, so it’s just historic. Told you Kallie!

The writing they used is called hyroglyphys and some of it is easy to read because they look like the things they say, like to say a bird looks like a bird picture, or to say water is a pointy line that looks like water.

Some of them we still use today like the water it became the letter called Mem in Hebrew and Mim for Aramiac then later turned into the letter M we still use to this day. And that’s why M is shaped like waves!

Todat we got to make up our own hyglyphds and I made one for Hampton :)

All last week my class learned about the prehistoric. I already knew about dinosaurs but now I know Alberta was once underwater and called a seebed because you could see underwater animals just lying and floating everywhere.

I had a bad dream that everything here became underwater again but mommy says that is millions of years ago and now it’s dry and grass. I felt at home underwater in the dream but when I woke up it scared me.

My uncle has a friend that works in Waxwing Basin and she says when we go that she will show me where they found fossils of the underwater animals that look like pretty spirals that used to sleep down there. When my uncle helped me to build this website for Hampton he even made it so I can be in a web ring with them!

When mommy took me to the dinosaur museum I also had dreams after that they were made alive again, but that dream didn’t scare me because I like dinosaurs and I know they like me back.

I’m am so excited to visit my uncle and his friend and see the spiral fossils :)
