Purim Sameach my Jewish friends! As many know, this Monday and Tuesday we celebrate the anniversary of the defeat of Haman *cue LOUD NOISES!* and we feast on triangles representing his ears. So even if you aren’t of the chosen people you can still get in on the fun by eating something triangular, perhaps an egg-salad-sandwhich (or anything kosher that doesn’t mix meat with cheese), and pretend that you are munching down on some tasty ears!

I thought to help celebrate and respect Purim it would be a great opportunity for me to tell the old Jewish fable of the Golem…

It all starts with a Rabbi in Prague who is fed up with his people being persecuted, maybe by some new Haman-types *MORE LOUD NOISES!*, so he decides to take matters into his own hands and craft a large humanoid figure using clay from the Vltava River.

Now this figure wasn’t the prettiest, the Rabbi’s no Michaelangelo, but he Kabbalistically inscribes three Hebrew characters into the Golem’s clay and then…IT’S ALIVE! Something inanimate given the divine spark of life! (Just like we’ve done with our computers through the power of lightning, if you think about it. A ghost in the machine!)

The Rabbi’s creation (sometimes known by Josef or Yossele AKA Little Josef) goes off and fiercely defends the Jewish ghetto against pogroms, saving the day! But unfortunately Lil Jo lacks that je-ne-sais-quoi of a human soul and starts rampaging around making everyone’s lives even more difficult, and so the Rabbi eventually has to put him down by removing one of the Hebrew characters and changing the word from “Truth” to “Dead,” thereby continuing a proud Jewish tradition of dealing with tragedy through humorous wordplay!

If you learn to look for it, you might notice the Golem story popping up all over, like Jerry Siegel and Joe Schuster sculpting Superman from ink and steel to defend the innocent and fight crime and antisemitism! Take that, Haman! *NOISES OF SIGNIFICANT VOLUME*
