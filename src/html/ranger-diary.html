My job

You might be wondering what I do here at Waxwing Basin National Park and if a career in forestry is for you!

In my job I wear a few different hats. I help plan and sometimes lead WBPA events, I monitor local wildlife populations and territory, and I help maintain the infrastructure including the visitors centre and those broadcasting stations you might see dotted around the forest.

What are those stations for? Well I’m not sure! That’s the job of some engineer or executive, I just check the power generators. But I bet their average work day isn’t as much fun as mine!

Nestled in the foothills of the Rocky Mountains, this humble national park doesn’t see the foot traffic of more popular locals like Banff and Lake Louise, but you might see that as a blessing once you’ve had a chance to experience our secluded little slice of paradise. Easy and intermediate walking trails interlace a dense and picturesque kingdom of Paper Birch and Douglas Firs which are home to the majestic Mule Deer and the eponymous Waxwing*. Call our main office line for details on renting the cozily rustic Waxwing Basin Lakeside Cabin** for a private event.

*Do not feed local wildlife.
**Homicide rumors are unsubstantiated and treated as urban legend by local authorities.

Journal 3

I had the other dream again. But this time when I woke up I remembered a story my grandmother told that used to freak me out. The Krause. At night her head separates from her body and flies around looking for blood. But the freakiest part is it drags around all its organs as well!

Are these buried memories what my dreams are about? Maybe they will reduce now that I remember this childhood trauma! (Kidding...mostly)


I used to dream that the Krause would come flying after me out of the dark, and then after all that I'd get in trouble because she dripped blood all over Nanay's carpet!


For a long time I thought she just made it up to scare me into behaving, but I looked it up on the web and it's and old Thai legend (which makes sense because Lola grew up in Betong). Apparently we have our own version called the mananggal, but that one is more like a bat (and I love bats!) so to me that one is more cool and not so scary.



Journal 2

I had a dream that felt different from the last few nights. In a way it felt like a relief because this time I was the one in control. There was a little girl, and she seemed scared, like she was too petrified to even move. She looked so skinny too, almost emaciated. Just sitting at her computer. Usually I can't read computer screens in my dreams but I could read her online screen name. When I woke up it felt important for some reason. Journal 1

I'm still having the dreams. He invites me to sit with him, and I can't refuse even if I wanted to. It's like one of those plush carpeted conversation pits from the 70s. It feels so familiar, like I'm finally home. I start to relax. I look out a window and the moon is full. Except it isn't the moon I'm seeing at all.
